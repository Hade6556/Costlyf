CostLyf Charter

We believe sharing subscriptions should be simple, affordable, and genuinely enjoyable.

That’s why we created this charter — a set of shared principles to help owners and co-subscribers enjoy their favorite services with confidence.

It all comes down to three things:
Kindness. Transparency. Mutual respect.

Subscription Owner 👑

Thank you for making sharing possible. A few simple commitments help keep everything smooth:

  • You must be the real owner
    Only the subscription owner can create a split. In some cases, an invoice may be requested for verification.
  • Make sure your plan supports sharing
    Your subscription must allow simultaneous users and match the service listed on CostLyf.
  • Fair pricing only
    Subscriptions must be shared at the official price in your country. CostLyf exists for fairness — not resale or profit.
  • Minimum 30 days of access
    Co-subscribers must receive at least 30 full days of access. If a split ends early, future payments are automatically canceled.
  • Trials must lead to real subscriptions
    Free trials can be shared only if they convert into a paid plan. Chaining free trials is not allowed.
  • Keep it secure
    Login details and invitation links must be shared only through CostLyf. Use the secure vault to protect credentials.
  • Respect the number of seats
    Only offer the number of spots your subscription allows.

Messages 💬

Messages exist to make communication easy and safe for everyone.

  • Be respectful
    Kind, polite communication is expected at all times — even when resolving issues.
  • Keep payments on CostLyf
    Never ask users to pay outside the platform.
  • Protect privacy
    Do not request or misuse personal information.
  • Be considerate with timing
    Avoid late-night or very early messages. Not everyone replies instantly — patience matters.
  • No discrimination
    Any form of discriminatory behavior will not be tolerated.

In short 😎

CostLyf is built on a simple idea: sharing should feel safe, fair, and human.

By following this charter, you help create a friendly community where everyone enjoys their subscriptions with peace of mind.

— The CostLyf Team
