FAQ

  • How do I get my login details? Open your subscription and tap "Reveal the details".
  • When is my next payment? Check the countdown on your subscription page.
  • Can I cancel my seat? Use the toggle "I will pay for the seat for the next month" to opt out before the next billing date.
  • Who do I contact for account issues? Contact the share owner first, or use Contact support for platform issues.
